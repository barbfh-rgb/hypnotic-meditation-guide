Introduction to Hypnotic Meditation
Hypnotic meditation is widely used for sport, stress management and healing. More and more people are learning and using hypnotic techniques as tools for self-improvement and change.

Learning hypnosis is something everyone can do — it is a natural state that we all slip in and out of frequently throughout each day. The trance state is already familiar to us all.

Can you think of a time when you were in your car and, on arrival at your destination, realised you had 'forgotten' parts of the journey? Have you ever been so completely absorbed in a book or film that time slipped away? That is the natural trance state.
How Do You Think?
Not everybody visualises. It is useful to be aware of how your thought processes work so that you can create successful hypnotic techniques that are relevant to you.

Visual thinkers — see pictures in their mind

Thinking thinkers — think of things without seeing images

Feeling thinkers — describe things by how they feel

Auditory thinkers — hear their thoughts or talk to themselves internally

All are equally valid. Adapt the techniques to suit the way you naturally think.
Relaxing
Begin to pay attention to your breathing. Try to make the out-breath last a little longer than the in-breath. Only do this in a way that feels comfortable for you.

As you begin to breathe more steadily, imagine a wave of relaxation washing over you and through you with every breath.

Begin to think of a comforting word — one that brings a sense of calm. Let this word gently repeat in your mind as you breathe out.
Deepening
The next stage is deepening. Some people are content to feel a little relaxed while others prefer to go deeper. Try a few ways to find which you prefer:

✦  Counting down from 10 to 1

✦  Imagining watching something floating down, such as a leaf

✦  Repeating words like "deeper and deeper"

✦  Saying a particular word to yourself as you breathe out, such as "calm"

When you are at a level that feels right, imagine you are in a special place. Make it as real as you can.
